# Kasper — Creative Agency

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, brand managers, startup founders, and portfolio visitors evaluating a studio's capabilities. They want visual confidence, originality, and a sense that this agency can deliver work that stands out.

## Product Purpose

Showcase a creative agency's identity, services, portfolio, and philosophy. Every section is designed to build trust in the studio's taste and execution. Success is a visitor thinking "this agency has vision" and reaching out.

## Brand Personality

Bold, restless, articulate. Speaks in confident declarative sentences. Uses color and space as voice. Never tries to be everything — it has a point of view and knows when to stop.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (big number + small label + gradient accent)
- The editorial-magazine reflex (italic serif display, ruled columns, monospace labels)
- Template-stock "modern creative agency" pages with identical card grids and Fraunces headings
- Overly safe white + teal corporate sites
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, beige backgrounds

## Design Principles

1. **Energy through contrast.** Bold scale jumps, deliberate color collisions, varied section pacing. Restraint is a tool, not the default.
2. **One idea per viewport.** Each fold has a single visual claim. No cluttered sections.
3. **Typography is the interface.** Hierarchy through weight and scale, not decoration. Every text element earns its size.
4. **Motion with purpose.** Entrances reveal structure; they don't decorate it. Stagger, not simultaneous. No bounce or elastic.
5. **Identity over convention.** If it looks like every other agency site, rewrite it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Focus states visible. Reduced-motion media query for all animations. Semantic HTML structure.
